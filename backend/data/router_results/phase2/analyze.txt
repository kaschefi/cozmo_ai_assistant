1. The Logit Collapse & "Semantic Attractor" Effect (ModernBERT vs. Qwen-2.5)

In the 20-tool setting, candidate tools retrieved by FAISS are generally semantically distinct (e.g. move_forward vs. weather_agent vs. spotify_play_playlist).

In the 100-tool setting, tool density produces intra-domain synonym clusters. When a user asks a lighting query, the candidate set provided to the classifier contains: ['set_light_color', 'lights_on', 'lights_off', 'set_light_brightness', 'activate_light_scene'].

When testing these live through laya.load("convaiinnovations/laya-typed-decisions"), the log results were:

text
Query: 'dim the room lights down to thirty percent'
  Expected: set_light_brightness (Rank 1 distance: 0.36)
  Candidates: ['set_light_brightness', 'lights_off', 'lights_on', 'adjust_screen_brightness']
  Laya Choice: lights_off (confidence: 0.05) -> WRONG
Query: 'change the smart lights color to warm orange'
  Expected: set_light_color (Rank 1 distance: 0.45)
  Candidates: ['set_light_color', 'set_backpack_lights', 'lights_on', 'lights_off']
  Laya Choice: lights_on (confidence: 0.02) -> WRONG
Query: 'switch the room to the reading light preset'
  Expected: activate_light_scene (Rank 1 distance: 0.55)
  Candidates: ['activate_light_scene', 'lights_on', 'lights_off', 'set_light_brightness']
  Laya Choice: lights_on (confidence: 0.05) -> WRONG
Query: 'turn up the display brightness to maximum'
  Expected: adjust_screen_brightness (Rank 1 distance: 0.45)
  Candidates: ['adjust_screen_brightness', 'set_light_brightness', 'lights_on', 'set_light_color']
  Laya Choice: lights_on (confidence: 0.06) -> WRONG
Why LAYA failed: ModernBERT is a 140M-parameter bidirectional encoder. When 5 candidates share the same core vocabulary (light, room, smart, turn, brightness), self-attention layers distribute weights evenly across the shared tokens. The final softmax classification head experiences entropy collapse: confidence values drop to 0.02 – 0.06. Because the margins are near zero, short, high-frequency pre-training labels (lights_on, lights_off) act as semantic attractors and dominate the output.
Why the LLM succeeded: qwen2.5:3b has 3 Billion parameters (21x larger) and operates as an autoregressive causal decoder. It performs attribute subsumption reasoning: it reads "warm orange" and determines that a specific color modifier requires set_light_color rather than the superordinate lights_on.
2. Negation Blindness & Entity Ambiguity in Bidirectional Attention

Cross-encoders evaluate text similarity via joint sequence representation without causal step-by-step reasoning tokens:

Query: "make the PC louder, not Cozmo's speaker"
Expected: set_system_volume
Candidates: ['set_robot_volume (0.42)', 'set_system_volume (0.53)', 'mute_system_audio (0.58)']
LAYA Choice: set_robot_volume (confidence: 0.02)
Root Cause: ModernBERT observes the strong bidirectional token link between "Cozmo" in the query and "Cozmo robot speaker" in the description of set_robot_volume. It cannot parse the negative scope ("not Cozmo's speaker"), causing it to select the negated entity.
LLM Behavior: The LLM's causal attention mask and instruction fine-tuning resolve the negation constraint, routing to set_system_volume.
3. Layer 1 Semantic Reflex Collisions (Desktop vs. Robot)

In the 20-tool setting, semantic_layer.py Tier 1 reflexes were designed around Cozmo's physical actions. In the 100-tool benchmark, desktop and system utilities were introduced, creating cross-domain collisions:

text
Query: 'put the laptop to sleep'
  Expected: put_computer_to_sleep
  Actual Predicted: dock_with_charger (Tier 1 Reflex)
Query: 'put the PC into sleep mode'
  Expected: put_computer_to_sleep
  Actual Predicted: dock_with_charger (Tier 1 Reflex)
Query: 'suspend the computer until tomorrow morning'
  Expected: put_computer_to_sleep
  Actual Predicted: set_alarm (Tier 1 Reflex)
Root Cause: The Layer 1 FastEmbed threshold for "go to sleep" was mapped to dock_with_charger. Because "put the laptop to sleep" shares high cosine similarity with "go to sleep", Tier 1 fired at 4.5ms and committed to dock_with_charger before Layer 2 RAG or LAYA ever saw the query.
